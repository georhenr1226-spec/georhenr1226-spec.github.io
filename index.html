Source: Startup Stumbles · Chapter 11 Watch ↗

What is collected

Publicly indexed Chapter 11 debtor-case metadata with a filing date between January 1 and the current date in 2026. Chapter 15 entries and duplicate docket IDs are excluded. Filing documents are linked, not hosted here.

What the numbers mean

Each distinct docket counts once. Affiliates can file separate cases, so one corporate restructuring may contribute many records. These counts do not measure the number of unique businesses or bankruptcy outcomes.

How industries are assigned

Source-provided categories are retained without inferring industries from debtor names. The source’s “Other” is shown as “Unclassified.” These are broad source labels, not verified NAICS codes.

Coverage and freshness

The source monitors selected major commercial districts and excludes consumer cases and some shell entities. Coverage and labels can be incomplete. The site checks the source on visits, cached for up to six hours, with a dated snapshot if collection fails. No missing record means “no filing.”

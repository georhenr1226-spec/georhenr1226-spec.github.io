07LIQUIDATION

Chapter 7

A potential route for eligible debts through a trustee-administered liquidation. Nonexempt property may be sold.

Who should explore it? +

Individuals and certain businesses may consider it. Consumer debtors may need means testing; an entity does not receive a Chapter 7 discharge. Exemptions and prior cases matter.

13INDIVIDUAL REPAYMENT

Chapter 13

A court-supervised repayment plan for eligible individuals with regular income, including some sole proprietors.

Who should explore it? +

Ask about a three-to-five-year plan and treatment of arrears. Debt limits, payment ability and plan confirmation require review. Separate business entities cannot file under this chapter.

11REORGANIZATION

Chapter 11

A framework to reorganize business obligations, pursue a sale or address a complex individual situation.

Who should explore it? +

Operating businesses and certain individuals may explore this route. Ask whether Subchapter V, a small-business election within Chapter 11, is available and appropriate.

12FAMILY FARMING & FISHING

Chapter 12

A specialized adjustment-of-debt process for qualifying family farming or commercial fishing operations.

Who should explore it? +

Ownership, regular annual income, debt levels and the share of income and debts tied to the operation all require a specialized eligibility analysis.

Municipal, regulated-financial and cross-border matters need specialist review. This tool flags them rather than treating them as a standard personal or business case.
